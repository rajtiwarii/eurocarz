FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything You Need to Know

What are the requirements for self-drive rental? ▼

Drivers must be at least 23 years of age with a valid government driver's license held for at least 2 years, along with photo identification (Passport or Aadhaar). International tourists are welcome with an International Driving Permit (IDP).

How does the security deposit work? ▼

A refundable security deposit is placed as an authorization hold or UPI pre-auth prior to vehicle handover. Upon return and standard vehicle check, the hold is released within 12 to 24 hours.

Can I request doorstep delivery to airports or private terminals? ▼

Yes, our white-glove team delivers vehicles directly to private jet terminals, VIP arrivals, luxury hotels, or your residence at your preferred time.
